LEGAL & PRIVACY

PRIVACY POLICY

Football President · PitchMind Games · Last updated: 27 October 2026

1. Developer and contact

Football President is an Android football-management game developed by PitchMind Games. For support or privacy requests, contact pitchmindgames@gmail.com.

2. Information stored in the game

The game stores career progress, club and president names entered by the player, team and player information, preferences and other game state locally on the device. Core offline career play does not require a PitchMind Games account or a developer-operated cloud-save service. If a custom data pack is imported, the selected file is used to provide the requested in-game content.

3. Advertising and information processed by Google

Football President includes Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) for banner advertisements, interstitial advertisements during appropriate game transitions, and optional rewarded video advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may collect and process IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), advertising or device identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostic information for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, security and — where permitted — personalization. See Google Privacy Policy and Google partner technologies.

4. Consent and privacy choices

Where required, the game uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to collect and manage advertising consent. Depending on device, region and consent requirements, an advertising privacy-options form can be accessible from the game's Settings → Legal & Privacy → Privacy Options. Rewarded advertisements are optional, and rewards are credited when completion is confirmed.

5. Support requests and diagnostics

If you contact pitchmindgames@gmail.com, we receive the email address and information you choose to send so that we can respond. The reviewed version has no separately identified developer-operated analytics or crash-reporting service, but the advertising SDK may process diagnostic data as described above. Any additional service added to a release may require an update to this policy.

6. Security and sharing

Local career saves are not intentionally transmitted to a PitchMind Games server during ordinary offline gameplay. Google and its advertising partners may receive advertising-related data and may process it in other countries under their own privacy policies. Reasonable measures are taken to protect information, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Retention and deletion

Local game information remains on the device until deleted in the game when supported, cleared through device settings or removed when the game is uninstalled. Device backup settings may affect whether some app data can be restored. Support messages may be retained as needed to respond and manage follow-up requests. Google and its partners control retention of the data they process.

8. Children

The game does not knowingly request personal contact information from children as part of the core career experience. Parents and guardians should review game advertisements and device settings for younger players. The game's Google Play audience and ad declarations must match its final published configuration.

9. Website and links

This website is hosted through GitHub Pages, which may process technical connection data subject to GitHub's policies. External services and links, including Google Play, are governed by their own policies.

10. Updates

This policy may change when game features, third-party services or relevant requirements change. The latest revision will appear at this URL with a new update date.

11. Contact

PitchMind Games · pitchmindgames@gmail.com.

Before the public launch, the developer should verify this text against the exact shipped SDKs, Google Play Data Safety declarations and active consent setup.
